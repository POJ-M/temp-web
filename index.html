The Vision of April 12, 2003

The prophetic encounter that birthed Presence of Jesus Ministry

On April 12, 2003, God mightily took hold of Pastor T. Inbakumar through the limitless power of His Holy Spirit. That evening, as he was praying earnestly in God's presence, the Lord opened a divine vision before him.

"Before me lay a mountain-like pile of rolled-up flex banners with verses written on them. As I opened them one by one to read, I saw that all the verses had been erased. Hoping that God was going to speak to me through the scriptures, I was disappointed. In that state of disappointment, God spoke to me clearly:"

"Each banner you see represents the human heart. The devil erases the scripture from their hearts so that the Word does not gain strength. Will you come to rewrite the scriptures that were erased from the hearts of My people?"

Yielding wholeheartedly to this clear calling voice, by God's grace, the ministry was initiated under the name "Presence of Jesus" (இயேசுவின் பிரசன்னம் ஊழியங்கள்) to carry out His work. Since that day, the Lord has directed our paths from Peikulam into 70+ surrounding villages and beyond.

Early Life & Heritage in Meerankulam

Pastor T. Inbakumar was born into a traditional Christian family in Meerankulam, a village in the Sathankulam Taluk of Thoothukudi District, Tamil Nadu.

From his childhood days, he held a deep affection for the Lord, loving the church, its fellowship, and worship. Although he attended church regularly and participated in every worship service, he lived without a clear understanding of personal salvation.

Bodily Illness, Healing & Divine Encounter (2003)

God's sovereign hand drew him closer through a prolonged physical illness in his body. In this vulnerable state of seeking healing and spiritual truth, he was guided in the Lord through Sis. A. Mary Pappa.

By God's magnificent grace, in the year 2003, Pastor Inbakumar received salvation and the mighty anointing of the Holy Spirit, transforming his life and purpose permanently.

Overcoming Hardship & Faithful Ministry

Thereafter, following God’s divine calling received in the vision of April 12, 2003, he accepted the call to full-time ministry. Overcoming severe family circumstances, opposition, and prolonged seasons of poverty, God has graciously enabled him to carry out His ministry with faithfulness, love, and tireless enthusiasm.

Today, he ministers across more than 70 villages, leads the Peikulam local church sanctuary, guides youth and children, hosts annual festivals, and extends missionary reach into tribal territories of North India.

Children's Ministry

"Let the little children come to Me, and do not forbid them; for of such is the kingdom of heaven." — Matthew 19:14

By God's abundant grace, the Lord continues to bless the children's ministry. Every Sunday, Sunday School classes for children are conducted from 12:00 PM to 1:00 PM. Through these classes, many children learn scriptures, action songs, and godly character.

Furthermore, every year during the school holidays, the Vacation Bible School (VBS) is conducted. Many children from surrounding villages participate in this VBS, accept Christ as their savior, and receive His blessings.

Village Gospel Ministry

"They preached the gospel in many villages... and returned to Jerusalem." — Acts 8:25

According to the scripture, "Preaching the gospel is a necessity laid upon me," gospel outreach is carried out every Sunday evening at 4:00 PM by distributing gospel tracts and New Testaments.

Good News Festival

By God's great grace, the Good News Festival is conducted every year during the second week of June across three days: Friday, Saturday, and Sunday.

Vehicles are dispatched to over 100 surrounding villages to bring people to these meetings. Thousands attend and receive true deliverance, salvation, and blessings that come through Jesus Christ.

Every year, during the first week of the first month (January), New Year Blessing Meetings are conducted for two days: Saturday and Sunday.
